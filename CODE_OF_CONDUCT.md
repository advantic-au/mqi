# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place to collaborate. Everyone participating
in project spaces, including issues and pull requests, is expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when disagreeing.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.
- Keep communication constructive and relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another
person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may restrict participation when
needed to address unacceptable behavior. Maintainers will consider the nature and impact of the
behavior and aim to respond fairly and proportionately.

To report a conduct concern, contact the project maintainer privately at
[warren@advantic.au](mailto:warren@advantic.au). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
